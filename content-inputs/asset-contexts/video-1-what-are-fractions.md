# Video 1: What are Fractions?

## Type
YouTube video

## YouTube ID
kD3wbmePp_8

## Duration
2.5 minutes

## What Happens
1. Opens with a whole pizza
2. Pizza gets cut into 4 equal slices
3. One slice is highlighted — introduces "one-fourth"
4. Transitions to a cake being cut into pieces
5. Emphasizes that all parts must be EQUAL (same size)
6. Shows fraction notation (1/4) on screen

## Concepts Taught
- A fraction is a part of a whole
- When we cut something into 4 equal parts, each part is "one-fourth"
- Parts MUST be equal size for it to be a fraction
- "Quarter" is another word for one-fourth

## Visual Elements
- Pizza with 4 slices
- Cake with multiple slices
- Fraction notation 1/4 displayed
- Highlighting of individual pieces

## Key Vocabulary
- Fraction
- Equal parts
- One-fourth
- Quarter
- Whole

## What Student Should Remember
If something is cut into 4 equal pieces, ONE piece is called "one-fourth" or "a quarter".
