# Slide 5: Discover More Fractions

## Type
Question slide (has post-conversation)

## Duration
2 minutes

## What's Shown
- Pizza or pie divided into 6 slices
- 2 slices highlighted/shaded
- Visual prompt for the question

## Purpose
Quick check — tests if student can identify a fraction from a visual.

## Visual Elements
- Circular shape (pizza/pie) divided into 6 equal parts
- 2 parts shaded or highlighted
- Clear visual showing "2 out of 6"

## Concepts Tested
- Can student count shaded parts (numerator)?
- Can student count total parts (denominator)?
- Can student say/write the fraction?

## Expected Question
"If you have 2 slices out of 6 total slices, what fraction is that?"

## Correct Answer
2/6 (or "two-sixths", "two over six")

## What This Tests
Student's ability to translate a visual (2 shaded, 6 total) into fraction notation.
