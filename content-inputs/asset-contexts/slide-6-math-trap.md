# Slide 6: Math Trap — Find the Error

## Type
Question slide (has post-conversation)

## Duration
2 minutes

## What's Shown
- Two bar diagrams side by side
- One labeled "2/4"
- One labeled "2/6"
- One of the diagrams is WRONG (doesn't match its label)

## Purpose
Diagnostic challenge — tests if student can spot an error in fraction representation.

## Visual Elements
- Two horizontal bar diagrams
- Shaded portions
- Fraction labels beneath each
- One diagram intentionally incorrect

## The Error
The 2/6 diagram does NOT show 6 equal parts — it might show fewer parts or unequal parts.

## Concepts Tested
- Can student verify if a diagram matches its fraction label?
- Does student understand that denominator = total equal parts?
- Critical thinking / error detection

## Expected Question
"Look at the two bar diagrams. One shows 2/4 and one shows 2/6. Which diagram is wrong?"

## Correct Answer
The 2/6 diagram is wrong (it doesn't show 6 equal parts)

## Why This is Valuable
Tests deeper understanding — not just reading fractions, but verifying correctness.
