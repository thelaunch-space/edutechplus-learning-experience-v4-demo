# Video 2: Bigger Fractions

## Type
YouTube video

## YouTube ID
pag3P1l3Tdk

## Duration
1.9 minutes

## What Happens
1. Reviews that fractions show parts of a whole
2. Introduces fractions where the top number is MORE than 1
3. Shows examples: 2/4, 3/6, 2/5
4. Explains that the top number counts how many pieces you HAVE
5. Visual examples with shaded pieces

## Concepts Taught
- The numerator (top number) can be bigger than 1
- 2/4 means you have 2 pieces out of 4 total
- 3/6 means you have 3 pieces out of 6 total
- The top number tells us HOW MANY pieces we have

## Visual Elements
- Shapes divided into pieces
- Multiple pieces shaded/highlighted
- Fraction notation with numbers > 1 on top
- Counting animations

## Key Examples
- 2/4 = two pieces out of four (two-fourths)
- 3/6 = three pieces out of six (three-sixths)

## What Student Should Remember
The TOP number tells you how many pieces you have. It can be 1, 2, 3, or any number!

## Key Vocabulary
- Numerator (reinforced)
- "Two-fourths"
- "Three-sixths"
