# Applet A3: Cake Fractions

## Type
Interactive HTML5 applet

## File Path
/fractions-module-content/applets/A3. M2-Fraction Statement Cake Snapshot/index.html

## Duration
3 minutes

## What Student Does
1. Sees a cake divided into pieces
2. Explores fraction vocabulary through the cake story
3. Learns the names for the top and bottom numbers
4. Interactive elements highlight numerator and denominator

## Interaction Type
- Story-based exploration
- Tapping to reveal vocabulary
- Visual highlighting

## Concepts Taught
- **Numerator** = the top number (how many pieces you have)
- **Denominator** = the bottom number (total pieces)
- Fraction vocabulary using cake as context

## Visual Elements
- Cake divided into pieces
- Fraction notation with labeled parts
- Arrows pointing to numerator and denominator

## Key Vocabulary Introduced
- **Numerator** — the top number, counts how many pieces
- **Denominator** — the bottom number, shows total pieces

## What Student Should Remember
The TOP number is called the NUMERATOR — it counts your pieces.
The BOTTOM number is called the DENOMINATOR — it shows total pieces.

## Example Used
In 3/4:
- 3 is the numerator (you have 3 pieces)
- 4 is the denominator (there are 4 pieces total)
