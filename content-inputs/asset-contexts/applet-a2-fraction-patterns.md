# Applet A2: Fraction Patterns

## Type
Interactive HTML5 applet

## File Path
/fractions-module-content/applets/A2.M2-Fraction Paper Cut Snapshot/index.html

## Duration
2 minutes

## What Student Does
1. Sees rectangles representing different fractions
2. Watches as rectangles are divided: 1/2, 1/4, 1/6
3. Can interact to see the cutting happen
4. Compares how many pieces each fraction has

## Interaction Type
- Visual comparison
- Tapping to see divisions
- Counting pieces

## Concepts Taught
- Different fractions have different numbers of pieces
- 1/2 = 2 pieces, 1/4 = 4 pieces, 1/6 = 6 pieces
- More cuts = more pieces
- The bottom number tells how many pieces total

## Visual Elements
- Side-by-side rectangles
- Animated cutting lines
- Piece count labels

## Key Comparison
**1/4 vs 1/6**:
- 1/4 has 4 pieces
- 1/6 has 6 pieces
- So 1/6 has MORE pieces than 1/4

## What Student Should Remember
The bigger the bottom number, the more pieces you have. 1/6 has more pieces than 1/4.

## Has Interactive Slide
Yes — after this applet, there's a dynamic interactive slide (FractionCompareSlide) that reinforces the 1/4 vs 1/6 comparison with tap interactions.
