# Slide 1: Why Fractions?

## Type
Narration slide (no question)

## Duration
1 minute

## What's Shown
- Colorful image of friends sharing a cake
- Fun, inviting visual to hook student interest

## Purpose
Motivation hook — get student excited about learning fractions by connecting to real life (sharing with friends).

## Key Message
"Ever tried sharing a cake with friends? Let's see how sharing cake helps us understand halves, quarters, and more!"

## Concepts Introduced
- Fractions relate to sharing
- Fractions are useful in real life
- This will be fun!

## Visual Elements
- Cake being shared
- Happy friends
- Playful, colorful design
