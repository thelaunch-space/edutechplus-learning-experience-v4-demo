# Applet A4: Advanced Practice

## Type
Interactive HTML5 applet

## File Path
/fractions-module-content/applets/A4.M2-Fraction Cut and Glue Practice 2/index.html

## Duration
3 minutes

## What Student Does
1. Creates fractions with numerators greater than 1
2. Divides shapes into parts (e.g., 5 parts)
3. Colors/shades multiple pieces (e.g., 3 out of 5)
4. Writes the fraction for what they created
5. Practices making 2/5, 3/5, 4/6, etc.

## Interaction Type
- Cutting/dividing shapes
- Coloring pieces
- Writing fractions
- Drag and arrange

## Concepts Taught
- Creating fractions with bigger numerators
- Counting shaded pieces → numerator
- Counting total pieces → denominator
- Writing fractions like 3/5

## Learning Goal
Student can create and identify fractions where the numerator is greater than 1.

## Key Examples
- Color 3 out of 5 pieces → 3/5
- Color 2 out of 5 pieces → 2/5

## What Student Should Remember
To write a fraction: count your colored pieces (top), count total pieces (bottom). Three colored out of five total = 3/5.

## Key Vocabulary Reinforced
- Numerator
- Denominator
- Three-fifths
- Two-fifths
